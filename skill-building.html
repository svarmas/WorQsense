Why this matters now

Courts no longer ask if you had a policy.
They ask if your process would withstand scrutiny.

PoSH is maturing

Weak investigations are now a documented liability. Regulators and courts are examining process quality, not just whether a policy exists.

Labour codes expand GRC

Structured grievance handling under the Industrial Relations Code now reaches far more employers. GRC members need the same rigour IC members require under PoSH.

Scrutiny is rising

In 2025, findings were overturned and respondents reinstated on process failures alone. The investigation process is now the evidence.

IMS Australia partnership

Investigative interviewing is a skill, not a personality trait.

In partnership with Interview Management Solutions (IMS, Australia). Evidence-based investigative interviewing training built for workplace, PoSH and employment relations settings. Not adapted from law enforcement. Designed for this work from the ground up.

PEACE Model

The internationally recognised structure for ethical, evidence-based investigative interviews: Preparation and Planning, Engage and Explain, Account, Closure, Evaluate.

Méndez Principles

Non-coercive, rights-respecting interview practice per the UN Special Rapporteur's framework on effective interviewing for investigations and intelligence gathering.

TILES System®

IMS's structured interview planning and record-keeping system. Pre-interview planning, in-interview execution, post-interview evaluation, logged as the interview happens.

In partnership with Interview Management Solutions (IMS, Australia). Led by Daren Jay: 30+ years in law enforcement investigations, UK Home Office Investigative Interview Trainer, lecturer at Charles Sturt University.

Why our training is different

We are investigators who train.
Not trainers who also investigate.

Participants practise with scenarios drawn from real investigations. Interviewing is practised by conducting interviews. Report writing is practised by writing reports. Capability is built by doing, not listening.
