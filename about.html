Practice philosophy

Independent. Structured. Evidence-led. Human.

Independent means being free to reach the finding the evidence supports, including an unwelcome one.

Independent

No advisory role, no prior relationship, no conflicting position. Willing to find what the evidence requires, including findings that are unwelcome.

Structured

Procedural fairness at every step: allegations put clearly, a real opportunity to respond, a record of how each decision was reached.

Evidence-led

Findings tied to evidence. Inferences documented as inferences. Credibility assessed on reasoning that can be read and tested, not on impression.

Human

Trauma-aware practice that accounts for how trauma affects memory, disclosure, and credibility. Honest scope, proportionate conclusions, no inflated findings.
